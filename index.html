OUR STORY · LESOTHO 🇱🇸

Stories have the power to connect a nation.

LesothoStream is a home for discovery: a carefully organised place to explore films, documentaries, creative work and educational content through one welcoming experience.

Inspired by the Kingdom in the Sky, the platform celebrates curiosity, culture, creativity and the many stories that deserve to be seen. It is designed around the needs of viewers in Lesotho while remaining open to audiences everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

What can I discover on LesothoStream?

Explore a curated catalogue of films, series, documentaries and educational selections. Each title includes basic information and, where available, a sample trailer link.

How do I save a title?

Choose “+ My List” on any catalogue card. The title will appear in your personal list, where you can search, sort or remove it.

Can I use LesothoStream on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and adjusts to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Are all videos hosted on this platform?

No. Some title details include links to third-party sample trailers. Availability and playback are controlled by those external platforms.

Can I suggest content?

Yes. Use the contact form to share a title suggestion or tell us what you would like to see included.

How do I manage my profile?

Use the account menu in the top navigation to view your profile details or sign out.
